# Layer 1: Macro Environment Analysis

> "In the short run, the market is a voting machine. In the long run,
> it is a weighing machine." — Benjamin Graham

## Purpose

Determine where we are in the economic cycle and what macro forces
are shaping the investment landscape. This sets the context for
everything else.

## Key Indicators to Check

### 1. Interest Rates & Fed Policy
- **Federal Funds Rate (DFF)**: Current level and direction
  - Rising rates → headwind for growth stocks, tailwind for banks
  - Falling rates → tailwind for growth stocks, bonds rally
- **10Y-2Y Treasury Spread (T10Y2Y)**: Yield curve shape
  - Inverted (< 0) → recession signal within 6-18 months
  - Steepening → economic recovery signal
- **Fed dot plot & meeting minutes**: Forward guidance tone

**Interpretation Framework:**
| Fed Stance | Impact | Favored Sectors |
|------------|--------|-----------------|
| Hawkish (tightening) | Negative for equities | Financials, Energy, Healthcare |
| Neutral (hold) | Stability | Broad market |
| Dovish (easing) | Positive for equities | Tech, Growth, Real Estate |

### 2. Inflation
- **CPI (CPIAUCSL)**: Headline and Core (ex food & energy)
  - < 2%: Deflationary concern → Fed may ease
  - 2-3%: Goldilocks zone
  - > 3%: Inflationary pressure → Fed may tighten
- **PCE Price Index**: Fed's preferred measure
- **Breakeven inflation rates**: Market expectations

### 3. Economic Growth
- **GDP Growth Rate**: Trend and acceleration/deceleration
- **ISM Manufacturing & Services PMI**:
  - > 50: Expansion
  - < 50: Contraction
  - Direction matters more than absolute level
- **Employment**: Nonfarm payrolls, unemployment rate (UNRATE)
  - Strong jobs + rising wages → potential inflation pressure
  - Weakening jobs → potential recession risk

### 4. Oil & Commodities
- **WTI Crude Oil**: Level and trend
  - < $50: Deflationary, hurts energy, helps consumers
  - $50-80: Balanced
  - > $80: Inflationary pressure, sector rotation trigger
- **Impact mapping:**
  - Oil up → Energy ↑, Airlines ↓, Industrials ↓, Consumer discretionary ↓
  - Oil down → Energy ↓, Airlines ↑, Retail ↑, Transportation ↑
- **Dollar Index (DXY)**:
  - Strong dollar → Hurts US exporters, helps importers
  - Weak dollar → Helps US exporters, commodities rally
- **Gold**: Fear gauge, inflation hedge

### 5. Market Regime
- **VIX (Fear Index)**:
  - < 15: Complacency (bull market)
  - 15-25: Normal
  - 25-35: Elevated fear
  - > 35: Panic / extreme fear → often contrarian buy signal
- **S&P 500 vs 200-day MA**: Bull/bear market regime
- **Market breadth**: Advance/Decline ratio
  - Narrow breadth in rally → fragile, few stocks driving gains
  - Broad breadth → healthy, sustainable trend

## Scoring Rubric

Rate the macro environment for equity investment:

🟢 **Favorable** (3 points): Fed dovish or neutral, inflation controlled,
   economy growing, VIX low, broad market breadth
🟡 **Mixed** (2 points): Some headwinds but manageable, mixed signals
🔴 **Unfavorable** (1 point): Fed hawkish, inflation high, recession risk
   elevated, VIX spike, yield curve inverted

## Sector Rotation Map

Based on the economic cycle phase:

```
Early Recovery → Financials, Consumer Discretionary, Industrials
Mid Cycle     → Technology, Communication Services, Materials
Late Cycle    → Energy, Healthcare, Consumer Staples
Recession     → Utilities, Healthcare, Consumer Staples (defensive)
```
